# The 2026-spec car (`carsim.params.f1_2026`)

Built to the 2026 F1 rules. The hard numbers come from the regulations as reported by Formula 1,
the FIA and the teams; the power-unit split and all aero coefficients are estimates, to be
calibrated against real 2026 lap times (`python tools/calibrate.py compare --year 2026 --all`).

| Item | 2026 | 2025 car in carsim | Source / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum mass, with driver, no fuel | 768 kg | 800 kg | FIA rules as reported by F1, ESPN (a few outlets give other figures) |
| Wheelbase (max) | 3.40 m | 3.60 m | F1, Red Bull "Bull's guide", Motor Sport |
| Width | 1.90 m | 2.00 m | same |
| Tyres | 18-inch, front 25 mm and rear 30 mm narrower | 18-inch | Pirelli/F1 coverage (one outlet: 15/10 mm, likely tread vs rim) |
| MGU-H | removed | 40 kW harvest | F1 |
| MGU-K | 350 kW deploy and harvest | 120 kW | F1, Motor Sport |
| MGU-K above 290 km/h | tapers to 0 at 355 km/h | none | several outlets (one: 280/340 km/h) |
| Energy | up to 8.5 MJ deployed per lap | — | reported; not enforced per lap in carsim yet |
| Combustion engine | ~400 kW | ~615 kW (+120 kW ERS) | estimate ("about 50/50") |
| Active aero | corner mode by default; straight mode (both wings flatten) in designated zones, for everyone | DRS (rear flap) | F1 beginner's guide, The Race, Motorsport.com |
| Straight mode | ClA and CdA × 0.55 | DRS: -0.10 CdA | estimate: ~55 % less drag was a pre-season prediction (ESPN) |
| Corner-mode trims (ClA, CdA) | low 2.9/0.78, medium 3.5/0.95, high 4.1/1.10 | 3.2/0.85, 4.0/1.10, 4.7/1.30 | estimates |

Not modelled yet: Overtake Mode (350 kW up to ~337 km/h + 0.5 MJ within 1 s of the car ahead: needs
other cars), the April 2026 change (250 kW outside the key acceleration zones), the per-lap energy
limit.

Aero zones (`carsim.aero_zones`) are found from the track shape (straights over 450 m with a radius
above 900 m), not from the FIA's list: Monza 3, Spa 2, Hungaroring 1, Monaco none (as in the real
rules); Singapore gets none, although the real one has zones (its straights curve slightly).
